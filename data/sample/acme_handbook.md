# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook

Company: Acme Robotics
Founded: 2014
Headquarters: Austin, Texas
CEO: Maya Chen

This handbook is the source of truth for employee policies at Acme Robotics. Use it when answering questions about time off, remote work, expenses, security, and promotions.

## Company overview

Acme Robotics designs warehouse automation robots. The company was founded in 2014 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas. The current chief executive officer is Maya Chen. The company operates one hardware lab in Austin and a software hub in Lisbon, Portugal.

## Time off

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) each calendar year. PTO is accrued monthly. Unused PTO may roll over up to 5 days into the next year. Sick leave is separate: employees receive 8 sick days per year.

Parental leave is 16 weeks of paid leave for the primary caregiver and 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver. Parental leave can be taken within 12 months of a child's birth or adoption.

## Remote work

Acme Robotics uses a hybrid model. Employees may work from home 3 days per week and must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Managers may approve a fully remote exception for roles that do not need lab access.

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 Central Time.

## Expenses

Employees may expense work purchases up to $75 without manager approval. Purchases above $75 require written manager approval before the purchase. Travel bookings over $500 require finance approval.

Submit expenses in the AcmePay portal within 30 days. Missing receipts over $25 will be rejected.

## On-call and compensation extras

Engineers on the production on-call rotation receive a $200 per week on-call stipend. A Sev-1 incident that lasts more than 4 hours also grants a $150 incident bonus.

The default company laptop is a Framework 13. Docking stations are provided for office desks.

## Security

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on every company account, including email, Git, and cloud consoles. Employees must use a password manager. USB storage devices are not allowed on lab machines. Report suspected phishing to security@acmerobotics.example within 1 hour.

## Promotions and reviews

Acme Robotics runs two promotion cycles each year: April and October. Calibration meetings happen in the first two weeks of those months. Employees need a current manager recommendation and at least 9 months in role to be eligible.

Performance reviews use a 4-point scale: Exceptional, Strong, Developing, and Needs Improvement.

## Facilities

The Austin office is at 4800 Manor Road, Austin, TX. Badge access is active from 07:00 to 20:00 on weekdays. After-hours access requires a security escort request submitted by 15:00 the previous day.
